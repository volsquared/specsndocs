# CIB Lending Platform
## Vision & Strategy
**Version:** 0.2 (Draft)

---

# 1. Vision

Build a workflow-agnostic Commercial & Corporate Lending Platform that transforms how lending decisions are made, supported and governed.

The platform will provide a single enterprise capability for credit data, financial intelligence, credit decisioning, lending lifecycle management and business insight, whilst integrating seamlessly with any upstream workflow or channel.

Rather than simply automating today's processes, the platform will enable a more intelligent, explainable and data-driven approach to commercial lending, allowing Relationship Managers and Credit Officers to focus on judgement rather than administration.

---

# 2. Why Change?

Today's lending landscape is constrained by fragmented data, manual analysis, disconnected systems and inconsistent decision processes.

Relationship Managers and Credit Officers spend significant time gathering information, validating data, performing repetitive financial analysis and producing documentation rather than exercising commercial judgement.

As lending volumes increase and regulatory expectations continue to evolve, the current operating model will become increasingly difficult to scale — and increasingly costly to run.

The future platform aims to fundamentally improve the speed, consistency, transparency and intelligence of the commercial lending process, materially reduce the manual effort required to support it, and maintain strong governance and regulatory compliance throughout.

---

# 3. Strategic Objectives

The platform will:

- **Reduce manual effort in credit analysis and documentation**, delivering a material FTE saving through automation of financial spreading, repetitive analysis and document production *(target to be quantified)*.
- **Improve decision speed and consistency** by executing deterministic, policy-driven decisioning where appropriate, and surfacing AI-assisted analysis and recommendations where human judgement benefits from additional insight.
- **Establish a single trusted source of lending data** — customers, facilities, limits, collateral, covenants and financial information — eliminating duplication and re-keying across systems.
- **Increase transparency and reduce regulatory/audit risk** through complete traceability, explainability and auditability for every lending decision.
- **Improve portfolio and operational visibility** through embedded analytics and business intelligence, supporting proactive rather than reactive credit management.
- **Future-proof the estate** by integrating with any enterprise workflow platform through well-defined APIs and business events, so innovation can be delivered without requiring changes to consuming workflow or channel applications.

---

# 4. Design Principles

The platform will be designed around the following principles:

- Business capabilities before technology.
- Workflow and channel agnostic.
- API and event driven.
- Deterministic where policy requires certainty.
- AI where intelligence adds measurable business value.
- Trusted data as a strategic asset.
- Explainability and audit by design.
- Composable and reusable across Commercial & Corporate Banking.

---

# 5. Scope

## In Scope
- Credit data foundation
- Financial analysis and spreading
- Credit intelligence
- Credit decisioning
- Limits and facilities
- Collateral management
- Covenant management
- Credit documentation
- Integration services
- Analytics and business intelligence
- Governance, evidence and audit

## Out of Scope
- User interface implementation
- Enterprise workflow orchestration
- Case management
- Task management
- Channel-specific presentation logic

These capabilities will remain the responsibility of upstream workflow and channel platforms, which will consume the lending platform through standard integration contracts.
