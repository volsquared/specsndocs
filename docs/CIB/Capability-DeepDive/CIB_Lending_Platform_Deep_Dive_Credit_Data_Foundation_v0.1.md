# CIB Lending Platform
## Capability Deep Dive

# Credit Data Foundation

**Version:** 0.1 (Draft)

---

# Purpose

Provide a single, trusted and traceable representation of all information required throughout the commercial lending lifecycle.

Credit Data Foundation exists so that every other capability in the platform — Financial Intelligence, Credit Decisioning, Lending Operations, Analytics — can rely on a consistent, authoritative view of customers, deals, facilities, financial statements, collateral, covenants and exposure, without each capability needing to source, validate or reconcile that information independently.

---

# Business Value

Credit Data Foundation reduces duplication, inconsistency and rework across the lending estate by establishing one authoritative source of lending information.

It enables:

- Trusted lending information available to every downstream capability
- Elimination of duplicate and conflicting records across systems
- Complete traceability of where information came from and how it has changed
- Faster, more reliable downstream analysis and decisioning
- Reduced operational effort spent reconciling data between systems
- A stable foundation that can absorb technology change without disrupting consumers

# Information Ownership

**Owns:**
- The authoritative record of customer, group, deal, facility, limit, collateral, covenant and exposure information
- Reference and external data as consumed and represented within the platform
- Historical snapshots and lineage of all of the above

**Does Not Own:**
- Financial analysis, ratios or derived intelligence (owned by Financial Intelligence)
- Credit decisions or decision rationale (owned by Credit Decisioning)
- Operational servicing activity — utilisation, reviews, documentation status (owned by Lending Operations)
- Workflow state, task assignment or approvals (owned by upstream workflow platforms)

# Integration Responsibilities

This capability should make trusted information available through stable platform integration contracts. Business responsibilities include:

- Provide trusted lending information to any authorised consumer
- Maintain consistency of information across all consuming capabilities
- Publish notification when owned information changes
- Support retrieval of current and historical information
- Support reconstruction of information as it existed at a given point in time

Implementation mechanisms (REST, Events, Messaging etc.) are intentionally defined within the Integration Architecture rather than here.

# Analytics

The capability should contribute information including:

- Data quality and completeness by domain
- Data lineage and provenance for any given record
- Duplication and reconciliation activity
- Coverage of the single lending identifier across the customer base
- Historical accuracy and stability of trusted records

# Design Principles

*(Specific to Credit Data Foundation only — see Capability Model "Capability Principles" for platform-wide rules already applying to every capability.)*

This capability shall:

- Own information once — no consuming capability re-derives or duplicates authoritative data.
- Treat information as temporal — history is retained, not overwritten.
- Make every change traceable to its source.
- Require consumers to consume trusted information, not silently reinterpret it.
- Establish trust before intelligence — nothing built downstream can be more reliable than the data it stands on.

---

# GenAI Value Proposition

Generative AI augments this capability by improving discovery of, and explanation about, trusted information — it does not become the source of that information.

Potential applications include:

- Natural language exploration of customer, deal and facility information.
- Explaining data lineage and provenance in plain language ("where did this figure come from, and when did it last change").
- Investigating and summarising data quality issues.
- Summarising a customer's or group's lending footprint across facilities and entities.

The authoritative record itself, its validation, and its lineage remain deterministic and fully auditable — GenAI sits on top of this capability to make it more accessible, it must never become the system of record.

# Success Measures

**Operational Measures**
- Data quality and completeness scores by domain
- Duplication / reconciliation incident rate
- Single lending identifier coverage across the customer base
- Time to resolve data discrepancies

**Business Measures**
- Reduction in manual re-keying and reconciliation effort across consuming capabilities
- Downstream confidence in lending data (as reported by Financial Intelligence, Decisioning, Lending Operations)
- Reduction in audit findings relating to data trust, lineage or traceability
