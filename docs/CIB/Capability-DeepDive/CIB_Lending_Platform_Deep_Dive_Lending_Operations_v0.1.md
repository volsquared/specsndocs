# Lending Operations

**Version:** 0.1 (Draft)

---

# Purpose

Support the ongoing operational management of commercial lending throughout the lifetime of the customer relationship — after, and alongside, decisioning.

Lending Operations owns the ongoing management of facilities, limits, collateral, covenants, documentation and reviews. It keeps the operational state of every facility and relationship current, monitored and traceable.

Lending Operations does not make credit decisions and does not perform financial analysis. Credit Decisioning decides; Lending Operations manages what happens to a facility and relationship once a decision has been made and throughout its life.

---

# Business Value

Lending Operations provides continuous operational control over the lending book, rather than treating approval as the end of the process.

It enables:

- Better operational control over facilities, limits, collateral and covenants
- Continuous management of lending relationships, not just point-in-time approval
- Reduced operational risk through consistent servicing
- Earlier identification of financial or covenant deterioration
- Better customer servicing through timely, well-managed reviews
- Improved operational efficiency in documentation and facility administration
- Improved portfolio management and visibility

# Capability Classification

| Classification | Role |
|---|---|
| Primary Role | Manages the ongoing operational state of facilities and lending relationships |
| Owns Data | Yes — owns operational state (facility, limit, collateral, covenant and documentation status); does not own customer master data, financial analysis or decision outcomes |
| Produces Decisions | No |
| Produces Insight | No (produces operational state and status; insight is generated by Analytics & Business Intelligence) |
| Consumed By | Analytics & Business Intelligence, Governance, Evidence & Control, External Workflow Platforms |

# Information Ownership

**Owns:**
- Operational state of facilities, limits, collateral, covenants and documentation
- Review schedules, outcomes and trigger events
- Operational history of managed facilities

**Does Not Own:**
- Customer or group master information (owned by Credit Data Foundation)
- Financial analysis or intelligence (owned by Financial Intelligence)
- The lending decision itself or its rationale (owned by Credit Decisioning)
- Workflow state, task assignment or approval routing (owned by upstream workflow platforms)

# Integration Responsibilities

This capability should make operational state and events available through stable platform integration contracts. Business responsibilities include:

- Manage facilities and limits
- Manage collateral
- Monitor and manage covenants
- Manage credit documentation
- Publish current operational status
- Notify consumers of operational events (e.g. breach, review due, documentation outstanding)

Implementation mechanisms (REST, Events, Messaging etc.) are intentionally defined within the Integration Architecture rather than here.

# Design Principles

*(Specific to Lending Operations only — see Capability Model "Capability Principles" for platform-wide rules already applying to every capability.)*

This capability shall:

- Keep operational state reflective of reality at all times, not just at decision or review points.
- Monitor continuously rather than only at scheduled review dates.
- Make every operational change traceable.
- Consume decisions, not redefine them — a decision outcome is a fixed input, not something operations reinterprets.
- Maintain a complete operational history for every managed facility.

---

# GenAI Value Proposition

Generative AI augments this capability by improving how operational information is surfaced and understood — it does not manage facilities or make servicing decisions itself.

Potential applications include:

- Summarise review packs for Relationship Managers and Credit Officers.
- Highlight covenant deterioration in plain language.
- Explain operational exceptions (e.g. why a facility is flagged).
- Assist with documentation review.
- Identify customers or facilities requiring attention.

Operational state itself, covenant testing and breach detection remain deterministic and fully traceable — GenAI sits on top of this capability to make operational information easier to consume, it does not determine operational status itself.
